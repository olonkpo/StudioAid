StudioAid

A small floating assistant for photographers, for Mac and Windows.

StudioAid sits on top of your editing apps as a small bubble. Open it to see your projects, track each step from booking to delivery, and run your studio without switching tools.

  • Project board with a status for every step, from booking to delivery and follow-up
  • Calendar, contacts, packages, contracts, quotes and questionnaires
  • Invoices you can customise with your own logo, colours and payment details
  • Reminder messages to clients by email or WhatsApp (you press send)
  • Your data stays on your computer, with optional private backup to your own Google Drive

Download for Mac or Windows
