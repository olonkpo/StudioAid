StudioAid privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

StudioAid is a desktop app for photographers to manage projects, clients, invoices and documents. This page explains what information the app handles and what it does with it. In short: your information stays on your own computer, and if you choose to sign in with Google, a backup is kept in your own Google Drive. We do not run servers that receive your data.

Information you enter

You type in projects, client names, email addresses, phone numbers, invoices, notes and similar details. This information is saved in a file on your computer, together with daily backup copies kept for 14 days. We, the developers of StudioAid, never receive it.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings, StudioAid asks for these permissions:

  • Your email address, only so the app can show which Google account is signed in.
  • Access to the app's private data folder in your Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission). StudioAid stores a single backup file there. This folder is hidden and can be used only by StudioAid. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files.

The backup file contains the same information described above (your projects, clients, invoices and settings). It goes directly between your computer and your own Google Drive. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot see it.

The sign-in token that lets the app reach your Drive is stored on your computer, using the operating system's secure storage where it is available.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, view, store or sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not share your information with third parties, other than your own Google Drive as described above.

Other network activity

  • Update checks. The app contacts GitHub to see whether a newer version exists and to download updates. No personal information is sent, but, as with any website, GitHub can see your internet address.
  • Email and WhatsApp buttons. These open your own mail app or browser with a message prepared for you. StudioAid does not send messages itself.

Your choices

  • You can use StudioAid without ever signing in to Google.
  • You can sign out in Settings at any time. This also revokes the app's access.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its hidden backup data in Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".
  • Deleting the app's data folder on your computer removes the local copy.

Your clients' information

You are responsible for how you use the client information you store in StudioAid, including following any privacy laws that apply to your business.

Children

StudioAid is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Google API Services User Data Policy

StudioAid's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
